Privacy Policy for Tally & Grow

Last updated: 9 July 2026

Tally & Grow ("the app") is a personal finance and investment tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Summary

Tally & Grow does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal or financial data. There is no account, no login, and no server backend operated by the developer. All of your financial data — accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, and investment holdings — is stored locally on your device only.

Data storage

All app data (accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, goals, investment holdings) is stored in a local database on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export a backup file yourself. Backup files you create are saved to your device's storage under your control; the developer has no access to them.

Network access

The app connects to the internet only to fetch public market data needed to show live prices for stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, and cryptocurrency, and to convert between currencies. These requests only ever contain a ticker symbol or currency code — never your name, account balances, transaction history, or any other personal or financial information. The services used are:

  • Yahoo Finance (stock/ETF price quotes)
  • CoinGecko (cryptocurrency prices)
  • mfapi.in (Indian mutual fund NAV data)
  • ExchangeRate-API (currency conversion rates)

These are third-party public data providers; the app does not send them any identifying information about you or your device.

Biometric app lock

If you enable the optional biometric lock, Tally & Grow uses your device's built-in biometric authentication (fingerprint/face unlock) via Android's standard BiometricPrompt API. Your biometric data is handled entirely by the Android operating system and is never accessed, seen, or stored by the app or the developer.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — used only for the live price and currency-conversion lookups described above.
  • Biometric — used only to unlock the app if you turn that feature on.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, or storage-wide permissions.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

Tally & Grow contains no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Children's privacy

The app is a general personal-finance tool and is not directed at children.
