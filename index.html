Privacy Policy for Tally & Grow

Last updated: 7 October 2026

App: Tally & Grow (com.tallyandgrow). Developer: Tally and Grow. Contact: anas.rizvi89@gmail.com.

Summary and local records

Tally & Grow is a local-first personal finance and investment portfolio tracker. The developer does not operate an account or financial-data backend. Accounts, balances, transactions, budgets, goals, holdings and locally calculated insights are stored in the app's private database on your device. The app has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not execute trades, link bank or broker accounts, move money, lend, or hold funds or cryptocurrency keys.

Optional Smart Transaction Capture

If you enable Smart Transaction Capture and grant Android's Receive SMS permission, the app examines new incoming SMS messages for likely purchase alerts. Parsing happens on your device. The draft may include amount, currency, merchant, card suffix and a suggested category. The complete SMS is not stored or uploaded. You must review and confirm the draft before it is saved as an expense. The app does not request READ_SMS or scan your existing inbox. Turn the feature off in Settings or revoke SMS permission in Android Settings to stop capture.

Network requests and external providers

Internet access is used to search supported investments, refresh market prices and obtain currency exchange rates. Requests can contain your search terms, ticker symbols, coin identifiers, mutual fund scheme codes or currency codes. They do not include account balances, transaction history, holding quantities, cost basis, SMS contents or app-lock credentials. Requests go over HTTPS to:

  • Yahoo Finance: stock and ETF search and quotes.
  • CoinGecko: cryptocurrency search and prices.
  • mfapi.in: Indian mutual fund search and NAV data.
  • ExchangeRate-API: currency conversion rates.

These providers receive the requested identifiers and ordinary network information such as your IP address and request headers. Requests may relate to investments you track. Providers handle their server logs under their own privacy practices; the developer does not receive those logs. Prices and rates may be delayed, cached or unavailable. Core record keeping can be used offline.

App lock

The optional PIN is stored as a salted PBKDF2 hash, not readable text. Optional fingerprint or face unlock uses Android's biometric authentication system; the app does not receive your biometric templates. App lock controls access to the app and does not mean that the local database itself is encrypted.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: public market-data and currency requests.
  • Use biometric: optional app unlock.
  • Receive SMS: optional purchase-alert capture from new messages, as described above.
  • Post notifications: reminders and alerts, including expense drafts ready for review. Financial notification content is marked private for the lock screen; Android notification settings also affect visibility.

The app does not request SMS-history access, contacts, location, camera, microphone or storage-wide access. Android's document picker provides access to files you choose for export or import.

Backups, CSV and sharing

You can export a password-encrypted backup and choose where Android saves it, including a local folder or a cloud document provider. The developer does not receive the backup. Keep the password safe; the app cannot recover it. CSV exports are plain, unencrypted files containing your selected financial records. Optional recap sharing sends the content you select to the app or destination you choose. Any chosen cloud provider or recipient handles exported or shared data under its own practices.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes the app's local database and settings. Exported backups, CSV files and shared copies remain in their chosen locations; delete those separately if needed. There is no developer-side user account to delete.

Children

The app is a general personal finance tool and is not directed at children under 13.
